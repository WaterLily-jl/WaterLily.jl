# Authors

WaterLily.jl was originally created by [Gabriel Weymouth](https://github.com/weymouth).
It has since been developed principally by Gabriel Weymouth,
[Bernat Font](https://github.com/b-fg), [Marin Lauber](https://github.com/marinlauber) and
[Tzu-Yao Huang](https://github.com/TzuYaoHuang), the *principal developers*, who coordinate its
development and can be contacted with questions about WaterLily.jl. In addition, *contributors*
have provided major additions or modifications. Together, these two groups form "The WaterLily.jl
Authors" mentioned in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

## Principal developers
