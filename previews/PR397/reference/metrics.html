Flow metrics ​

These are the functions that turn a flow field into a number. Most are cell-wise: they take a CartesianIndex and the velocity field and return the value at that cell, so they are used inside an @inside loop writing into a scratch array (usually sim.flow.σ).

The forces and moments are the exception: they integrate over the immersed surface and return a vector directly.

Forces and moments ​

Surface integrals over the immersed body, computed from the same kernel moments the solver uses to apply the no-slip condition. pressure_force(sim) and friends take the force on the whole of sim.body; pass a flow and a sub-body to get the force on one part of a composite geometry.

Time averaging ​

A MeanFlow accumulates the running mean of the pressure and the velocity as the simulation steps and, with uu_stats=true, of the velocity products too, from which uu returns the Reynolds stresses.

WaterLily.MeanFlow Type
julia
MeanFlow{T, Sf<:AbstractArray{T}, Vf<:AbstractArray{T}, Mf<:AbstractArray{T}}

Holds temporal averages of pressure, velocity, and squared-velocity tensor.
