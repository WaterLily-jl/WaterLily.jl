Flow and time stepping ​

A Flow holds the primary variables (the face-centred velocity u and the cell-centred pressure p) along with the immersion kernel moments measured from the body and the time-step history Δt.

mom_step! is the integrator: a two-stage fractional step, each stage computing convection and diffusion, applying the Boundary Data Immersion Method forcing, and then projecting the velocity onto a divergence-free field with the pressure solver. mom_predict!, mom_correct! and mom_project! are its pieces, exposed so that a custom time integrator can reuse them.

WaterLily.Flow Type
julia
Flow{D::Int, T::Float, Sf<:AbstractArray{T,D}, Vf<:AbstractArray{T,D+1}, Tf<:AbstractArray{T,D+2}, Lf, P<:NTuple, UBC<:Union{NTuple{D,Number},Function}, G<:Union{Function,Nothing}}

Composite type for a multidimensional immersed boundary flow simulation.

Flow solves the unsteady incompressible Navier-Stokes equations on a Cartesian grid. Solid boundaries are modelled using the Boundary Data Immersion Method. The primary variables are the scalar pressure p (an array of dimension D) and the velocity vector field u (an array of dimension D+1).

WaterLily.conv_diff! Method
julia
conv_diff!(r,u,Φ,λ;ν=0.1,perdir=())

Accumulate the convective and diffusive momentum flux of the Navier-Stokes equations

julia
r = (u⋅∇)u - ν∇²u

for the velocity field u into r.

  • r: convective and viscous force vector, overwritten.

  • u: velocity field to take the fluxes of.

  • Φ: dummy work array.

  • λ: convective interpolation scheme λ(u,c,d), e.g. quick, cds or vanLeer.

  • ν: kinematic viscosity.

  • perdir: tuple of periodic directions.

WaterLily.mom_predict! Method
julia
mom_predict!(a::AbstractFlow, t₀, t₁; udf=nothing, kwargs...)

Predictor phase of mom_step!: advect under u⁰ with the convective scheme a.λ and apply BDIM. t₀ and t₁ are the start and end times of the step; the domain BCs are enforced at t₁ by mom_project!.

source
WaterLily.mom_project! Method
julia
mom_project!(a::AbstractFlow, b::AbstractPoisson, w::Int, t; kwargs...)

Projection phase of mom_step!: solve the pressure Poisson equation, correct the velocity by Δt/w·∇p, and enforce BCs at time t. w=1 in the predictor and w=2 in the corrector. On return a.u is divergence-free and BC-consistent. kwargs are passed to solver!.

WaterLily.mom_step! Method
julia
mom_step!(a::AbstractFlow,b::AbstractPoisson;udf=nothing,kwargs...)

Integrate the Flow one time step using the Boundary Data Immersion Method and the AbstractPoisson pressure solver to project the velocity onto an incompressible flow. kwargs are passed to udf and poisson_kwargs to solver!.

WaterLily.udf! Method
julia
udf!(flow::AbstractFlow,udf::Function,u,t)

User defined function using udf::Function to operate on flow::AbstractFlow during the predictor and corrector step, in sync with time t. u is the velocity field the convective flux is evaluated on in that phase (a.u⁰ in the predictor, a.u in the corrector). A udf that needs the advecting velocity (eg. SGS) should use force!(flow,u,t; kwargs...). Keyword arguments must be passed to sim_step! for them to be carried over the actual call.
